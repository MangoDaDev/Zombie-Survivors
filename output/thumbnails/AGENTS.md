# Thumbnail Generation Rules

- Inspect and follow the exact character and creature references in `references/`; do not replace them with generic approximations.
- When orbiting blades are shown striking enemies, make the action physically coherent: place each hilt toward the orbit center, point each blade tip outward toward its target, and align impact effects with that same radial path.
- For high-CTR discovery thumbnails, keep one dominant readable hero, use large edge-framing threats and strong color contrast, minimize empty space, and preserve a clear one-second visual story.
